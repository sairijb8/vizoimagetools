Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Vizo Image Tools app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Khusairi (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Your Images

All image processing in the Application (compression, resizing, cropping and background removal) happens entirely on your device. The Service Provider does not upload, store or share your images on any server. Edited images are saved only where you choose (the Download/VizoImageTools folder on your device) or shared through apps you select. Temporary files are kept in the Application's cache on your device.


On-Device Machine Learning

The background removal feature uses Google ML Kit Selfie Segmentation, a machine learning model that runs entirely on your device. Your images are not sent to Google or any other party for this processing.


Permissions
  • Photo selection: the Application only accesses the images you pick through the system photo picker.
  • Storage (Android 10 and older only): used to save edited images to your Download folder.
  • Internet: used only to load advertisements.


Information Collection and Use

The Service Provider does not collect, store or sell any personal information. The Application has no account, login, form or analytics of its own, and does not ask you to provide personally identifiable information.


The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

  • Your device's advertising ID

  • Interactions with ads, such as views and clicks

This information is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Your Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Application asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy settings" button in the Application.

On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). You can also stop all data collection by the Application by uninstalling it.


Disclosure

Because the Service Provider does not collect personal information, it has none to disclose. Information collected by Google for advertising is subject to Google's policies linked above.


Data Retention

The Service Provider does not retain any of your data. Images and temporary files stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the Application. For questions about data held by Google for advertising, see the Google Privacy Policy above.
